# FossieGearScore 1.1.0

Gear scoring for **WoW Forever**. **GS** rates equipment by its stats and effects.
**DGS** adjusts that score for your class and talents.

## Install

Place the `FossieGearScore` folder in `Interface/AddOns` and enable it in-game.

## Use

- Hover items for scores, set bonuses, enchantment points and gear comparisons.
- View equipped totals on character and inspect panels, or hover a player.
- Use the talent dropdown to preview gear for another spec.
- Right-click your own portrait or a player's target portrait and select
  **GearScore** for equipment highlights, enchantment coverage, level and dungeon
  ratings, and spec-fit suggestions.

## Settings

Open `/fgs settings` or click the minimap button. Choose which scores to show,
combine or separate enchantment points, and adjust menu opacity.
Drag the minimap button to move it; use `/fgs minimap` to hide or show it.

DGS requires level 10 and spent talent points. Scores and dungeon benchmarks are
estimates, not entry requirements or measures of player skill.
